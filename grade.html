How to read this: the grade is an indicative signal built from public-record data — the number of later patents that cite this one (a common proxy for influence/value), how many years of term remain, and the legal status. Forward-citation counts come from the EPO's public database and can lag or undercount very recent citations. It is not a valuation, a validity opinion, or legal advice — treat it as a conversation-starter and confirm anything important with your patent attorney.
