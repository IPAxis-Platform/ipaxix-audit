A global electronics group runs its US and European portfolio through four different outside firms. Every deadline lives in a different attorney's docket and a shared spreadsheet that's always slightly out of date — and the team only hears about a response deadline when an email lands.

With IP Axix one board shows every deadline across all four firms, pulled straight from the USPTO and EPO. The team sees what's due before any attorney emails, nothing slips through the gap between firms, and when an examiner acts the response deadline appears the same day.

A patent firm dockets hundreds of cases across 25 clients by hand, and spends the week answering "what's coming up on our portfolio?" emails — each one a manual look-up across systems.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How does setup work?

You paste your patent or application numbers — US and European. IP Axix pulls the biblio, status, prosecution history and deadlines straight from the USPTO and EPO. There's no spreadsheet import and no data-entry project; the board builds itself in minutes.

Does it cover both US and European patents?

Yes. Status, dates and deadlines come live from the USPTO (US) and the EPO (European) — including US maintenance fees and EPO renewal fees, calculated against the real statutory rules.

Is this a replacement for my patent attorney?

No. IP Axix is a docketing and monitoring tool that makes sure a date is never missed. It isn't legal advice — always verify critical dates and actions against the official record with your patent attorney.

How is my data handled?

The free tools run on the public patent-office record and don't tie a lookup to you. If you ask us to monitor a portfolio, your data is used only to run your account and send your reminders — never sold or shared. You can export or delete it whenever you want.

How do I get access to the platform?

The platform is set up with you personally. Book a 20-minute walkthrough — we'll load your portfolio live on the call so you see it working on your own patents before you decide.
